Podcast Weave

Watch a real podcast episode from its creator's own YouTube upload. At a few moments the picture freezes, the way it would if someone hit pause, and AI echoes of the people in the room (and a guest or two) talk about what was just said. Then the original carries on from the same second.

The original is never copied or re-uploaded. Its views and ads stay with its creator. Only the new pause segments are ours, in a separate video.

What's real and what isn't

Everything in the pause segments is AI-generated. Every voice there is an AI echo, not the real person, and none of it was said by them. Each pause says so on screen and names the model behind the echoes. If you're one of the people echoed and want a weave taken down, open an issue on this repository and it comes down.

Make your own

  1. Pick an episode on YouTube and the moments where you'd pause it.
  2. Make one "segments" video with your intro, each pause, and a sign-off, and upload it to YouTube (unlisted is fine).
  3. Add weaves/<originalVideoId>.json listing the steps, and a folder <originalVideoId>/ with a copy of the page. The README has the template.
