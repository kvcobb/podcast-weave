Podcast Weave

Watch a real podcast episode from its creator's own YouTube upload. At a few moments the picture freezes, the way it would if someone hit pause, and AI echoes of the people in the room, plus a guest or two, talk about what was just said. Then the original carries on from the same second.

The original is never copied or re-uploaded. Its views and ads stay with its creator. Only the new pause segments are ours, in separate videos.

What's real and what isn't

Everything in the pause segments is AI-generated. Every voice there is an AI echo, not the real person, and none of it was said by them. Each pause says so on screen and names the model behind the echoes. Harmony and Joscha are AI seats, and Joscha's voice is borrowed from public recordings of Joscha Bach, who is not speaking. If you're one of the people echoed and want a weave taken down, open an issue on this repository and it comes down.

Make your own

  1. Clone the repository and follow AGENTS.md, or hand it to your coding agent.
  2. One pause: python -m cpc ingest, souls, me, voices, pause, render, companion, weave.
  3. Several pauses on several models: python -m cpc grid init, then grid next one pause at a time. Read each room before you accept it.
