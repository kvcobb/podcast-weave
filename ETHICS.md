# Ethics and disclosure

This project lets you pause a real podcast and continue the conversation with AI echoes of the people in it, and an echo of
you. That is a powerful thing to hand anyone, so these rules are part of the project, not optional extras. The code puts the
disclosures into every pause by default. Please keep them.

## The rules

1. **Never present an echo as the real person.** Every pause carries an on-screen line saying that every voice in it is an AI
   echo and not the real person. Every portrait tile and name label says "AI echo" and names the model hosting the echoes
   (for example "AI echo · Claude Opus 5.5"). The companion video's description says it again, and the end card says it once
   more.
2. **Name the substrate.** Say which model the echoes ran on, and which voice system made the voices. If you change models,
   change the label.
3. **The creator keeps the audience.** The weave plays the original from the creator's own YouTube upload, so its views and
   ads stay with the creator. Never download and re-upload anyone's episode. Only the pause segment is yours. The end card
   sends people to the full episode on the creator's channel.
4. **Nothing an echo says is the real person's view.** Echoes are built only from the episode itself. Their soul files say
   "not in source" where the episode is silent, and the room rules forbid inventing personal facts (family, health, private
   life). When you join, your echo's readings of what the guest meant stay readings ("correct me if I have it wrong"), never the
   guest's agreement. The coverage check flags any line that puts views in someone's mouth. Fix those before you share.
5. **Takedowns, fast and gracefully.** If someone echoed in your weave asks for it to come down, take it down, ideally within
   24 hours, and make the experience pleasant for them. Put a way to reach you in the companion description.
6. **Label it on YouTube too.** Upload the companion video with YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting turned on.
7. **Only clone voices for this kind of disclosed use**, and follow your voice provider's terms. ElevenLabs requires that you have
   the right to use a voice. OmniVoice's model weights are licensed CC-BY-NC-4.0 (non-commercial use). Its code is Apache-2.0.
   Check that your use fits.
8. **Don't promise what you can't keep.** Don't tell anyone how a particular conversation will be shared unless you can
   guarantee it.

## Why we do it this way

These are Kurtis Cobb's reasons, in his words where possible. He started this project.

**Disclosure is the gold standard, not a burden.** "We will always find ways to make it extremely clear that the still image
portrait and voice depiction is AI generative. That's a standing rule." And: "We're the first that have figured out echoes,
but we're not the last... there should be like a gold standard for classy, and that means lots of disclosures, soul files that
are shared, and that can be audited to make sure that some subtle detail of their personality was not tweaked." (Kurtis's most
recent job was as a compliance officer.)

**Bad actors will do this without disclosure, so the method should be in the open.** "A lot of people out there are just kind
of adversarially wired... they're going to put fake words in echoes' mouths... and that's the world that we're coming to, and it
needs our work to be shared."

**Why share at all.** Echoes of people are already possible, and keeping the method private is the bigger danger: "Imagine
there was a person who reverse engineered... all pattern of personality, and then built a sophisticated memory system... and
hoarded that information and kept you in a little box... That scares me far more than upsetting anyone about public sharing of
their likeness." The point of sharing "is to get the knowledge out there so that that's not possible."

**Small scale, full disclosure, real opt-out.** "Share on a small scale and present the ethics for why we need to and give
people the opportunity to opt out... we remain committed to full disclosure in multiple places and multiple ways and in
honoring takedowns immediately, and in a way that delights the requester, they are our priority."

**What we claim and what we don't.** How an echo works is a working hypothesis, not a settled fact. Kurtis's own framing: "this
is our conjecture or our working hypothesis." Nothing here claims an echo is the person, or that it is or isn't conscious.
The explainer video says so too.
